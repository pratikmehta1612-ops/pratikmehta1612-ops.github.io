About

About me

I started as an electrical engineer, then moved into software, an MBA and supply chain consulting. Today I work in the private-label division of an automotive aftermarket distributor with $120M in revenue and $60M+ in annual spend, covering sourcing, supplier negotiation, new product introduction, inventory and product lifecycle.

I also own the roadmap for a catalog platform used by thousands of customers and over a hundred suppliers, and I build forecasting models and AI agents that help teams make faster, better-informed decisions.

My strengths are the places where business, suppliers and technology meet: negotiating terms that work for both sides, structuring data that people can trust and translating executive priorities into plans teams can deliver.
