About me

I started as an electrical engineer, then moved into software, an MBA and supply chain consulting. Today I lead private-label accessories and towing categories in the automotive aftermarket, covering sourcing, supplier negotiation, new product introduction and product lifecycle.

I also own the roadmap for a catalog management portal used by thousands of customers and over a hundred suppliers, and I build forecasting models and AI agents that help teams make faster, better-informed decisions.

My strengths are the places where business, suppliers and technology meet: negotiating terms that work for both sides, structuring data that people can trust and translating executive priorities into plans teams can deliver.
