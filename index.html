About

About me

I started as an electrical engineer, entered professional journey with an software company then moved to manufacturing, did an MBA and supply chain consulting. Today I work in the private-label division of an automotive aftermarket distributor with $120M in revenue and $60M+ in annual spend, covering sourcing, supplier negotiation, new product introduction, inventory and product lifecycle.

My work spans the full cycle: forecasting demand, running RFI/RFQ/RFP processes, negotiating contracts and tooling terms, launching new products with suppliers and engineering, and retiring products in a way that protects cash. I also own the roadmap for a catalog platform used by thousands of customers and over a hundred suppliers, and I build forecasting models and AI agents that help teams plan with better information.

I make decisions with data: total cost of ownership models, forecasts and dashboards, and I bring suppliers, engineering, finance and senior leaders onto the same page. My strengths are the places where business, suppliers and technology meet: negotiating terms that work for both sides, structuring data that people can trust and turning executive priorities into plans teams can deliver.
